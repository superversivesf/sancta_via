# Mystery Images

This directory should contain images for the 20 Mysteries of the Rosary.

## Naming Convention

- `joyful_01.png` - The Annunciation
- `joyful_02.png` - The Visitation
- `joyful_03.png` - The Nativity
- `joyful_04.png` - The Presentation
- `joyful_05.png` - Finding in the Temple

- `sorrowful_01.png` - Agony in the Garden
- `sorrowful_02.png` - Scourging at the Pillar
- `sorrowful_03.png` - Crowning with Thorns
- `sorrowful_04.png` - Carrying of the Cross
- `sorrowful_05.png` - The Crucifixion

- `glorious_01.png` - The Resurrection
- `glorious_02.png` - The Ascension
- `glorious_03.png` - Descent of Holy Spirit
- `glorious_04.png` - The Assumption
- `glorious_05.png` - Coronation of Mary

- `luminous_01.png` - Baptism in Jordan
- `luminous_02.png` - Wedding at Cana
- `luminous_03.png` - Proclamation of Kingdom
- `luminous_04.png` - The Transfiguration
- `luminous_05.png` - Institution of Eucharist

## Image Specifications

- Format: PNG with transparency
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 or 4:3
- Minimum resolution: 800x600
- Style: Traditional religious artwork
