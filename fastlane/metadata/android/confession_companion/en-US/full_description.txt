Sancta Via Confession Companion helps Catholics prepare for the Sacrament of Reconciliation through a structured examination of conscience.
KEY FEATURES
- Comprehensive Examination of Conscience: Browse examinations organized by the Ten Commandments and by vocation (lay faithful, religious, etc.), helping you examine your life thoroughly before confession.
- Detailed Guidance: Each examination provides thoughtful questions and reflections rooted in Catholic moral teaching to help you identify sins and areas for spiritual growth.
- Favorites: Bookmark examinations you return to frequently for quick access before each confession.
- Recently Viewed: Pick up where you left off with a list of recently viewed examinations.
- Liturgical Calendar Integration: The app's theme follows the Catholic liturgical seasons — purple during Advent and Lent, white for Christmas and Easter, and green for Ordinary Time.
- Beautiful Interface: Clean, distraction-free design with adjustable font size and light/dark themes.
- Offline and Private: All content is stored locally. No internet connection needed. Your examination history never leaves your device. No accounts, no tracking, no data collection.
PRIVACY
Sancta Via apps collect no personal data. Everything is stored locally on your device. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. See our privacy policy for details.
