Sancta Via Prayer Companion is your daily companion for Catholic prayer, offering a rich collection of traditional prayers and devotions.
KEY FEATURES
- Extensive Prayer Library: Browse hundreds of traditional Catholic prayers, including:
  - Traditional prayers (Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be, Apostles' Creed, Act of Contrition, Morning/Evening prayers)
  - Litanies (Sacred Heart, Loreto, St. Joseph, Holy Spirit)
  - Marian antiphons (Salve Regina, Alma Redemptoris Mater, Ave Regina Caelorum, Regina Caeli)
  - Devotional prayers (Divine Praises, Daily Examen, Anima Christi)
  - Liturgical prayers (Kyrie, Asperges, Vidi Aquam, Benedicite)
  - Seasonal prayers (O Antiphons, Christmas Proclamation, Veni Sancte Spiritus)
  - Communion and confession prayers
- Favorites: Bookmark your most-loved prayers for instant access.
- Recently Viewed: Quickly return to prayers you've read recently.
- Search: Find prayers by name or keyword.
- Liturgical Calendar Integration: The app's theme follows the Catholic liturgical seasons — purple for Advent and Lent, white for Christmas and Easter, red for Pentecost and martyrs, and green for Ordinary Time. Rose for Gaudete and Laetare Sundays.
- Copy and Share: Copy any prayer to clipboard or share with friends and family.
- Beautiful Interface: Clean, contemplative design with adjustable font size and light/dark themes.
- Offline and Private: All prayer content is stored locally. No internet connection needed. No accounts, no tracking, no data collection.
PRIVACY
Sancta Via apps collect no personal data. Everything is stored locally on your device. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. See our privacy policy for details.
