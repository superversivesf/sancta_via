Sancta Via Liturgical Calendar is a focused, beautiful Catholic calendar app that helps you live the Church year through every season.
KEY FEATURES
- Full Liturgical Calendar: View the complete Catholic liturgical calendar, including all feast days, memorials, optional memorials, and ferias. Navigate month by month with ease.
- Daily Liturgical Information: Tap any day to see the liturgical celebration, rank (solemnity, feast, memorial), liturgical color, and relevant saint or feast information.
- Liturgical Seasons: The app's theme automatically changes with the liturgical seasons — purple for Advent and Lent, white for Christmas and Easter, red for Pentecost and martyrs, green for Ordinary Time, rose for Gaudete and Laetare Sundays.
- Vibrant and Muted Color Intensities: Choose between vibrant liturgical colors or a more muted palette for a calmer aesthetic. Both options respect the Church's color traditions.
- Today View: Instantly see today's liturgical celebration, color, and season when you open the app.
- Go to Today: One tap returns you to the current date from anywhere in the calendar.
- Beautiful Interface: Clean, contemplative design with smooth month navigation, light and dark themes, and adjustable font size.
- Offline: All calendar data is bundled with the app. No internet connection required. No accounts, no tracking, no data collection.
PRIVACY
Sancta Via apps collect no personal data. Everything is stored locally on your device. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. See our privacy policy for details.
