Sancta Via Bible Reading is a Catholic Bible app designed to help you engage with Sacred Scripture in harmony with the liturgical life of the Church.
KEY FEATURES
- Multiple Bible Translations: Read from the Catholic Public Domain Version (CPDV) and other Catholic-approved translations, with easy switching between them.
- Liturgical Calendar Integration: The app's theme changes throughout the Church year — purple for Advent and Lent, white for Christmas and Easter, red for Pentecost and martyrs, green for Ordinary Time, and rose for Gaudete and Laetare Sundays.
- Reading Plans: Follow structured reading plans to read through the Bible systematically, with progress tracking and daily reminders.
- Book and Chapter Navigation: Browse all 73 books of the Catholic Bible, including the deuterocanonical books (Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch, 1 & 2 Maccabees). Jump to any chapter with a tap.
- Verse Selection: Select and highlight individual verses for deeper meditation and easy sharing.
- Quick Lookup: Search by book name or reference (e.g., "John 3:16") to instantly find any passage.
- Continue Reading: Pick up exactly where you left off. The app remembers your last position in every translation.
- Beautiful Reading Experience: Adjustable font size, readable typography, and smooth scrolling optimized for extended reading sessions. Light and dark themes.
- Offline: All Bible text is bundled with the app. No internet connection required. No accounts, no tracking, no data collection.
PRIVACY
Sancta Via apps collect no personal data. Everything is stored locally on your device. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. See our privacy policy for details.
