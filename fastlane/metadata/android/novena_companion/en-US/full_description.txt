Sancta Via Novena Companion guides you through traditional Catholic novenas — nine days of prayer for a particular intention, rooted in centuries of devotion.
KEY FEATURES
- Traditional Novenas: A curated collection of classic Catholic novenas, including Marian novenas (Our Lady of Lourdes, Fatima, Immaculate Conception, Perpetual Help), saint novenas (St. Monica, St. Rita, St. Padre Pio, St. Francis), the Holy Spirit novena, and novenas for All Souls and the dead.
- Day-by-Day Guidance: Each novena is broken into nine daily prayers with clear day indicators so you always know where you are in the novena.
- Prayer Reminders: Set daily notifications to remind you to pray your novena at your chosen time. Never miss a day.
- Favorites: Save your favorite novenas for quick access. Start a novena anytime.
- Liturgical Calendar Integration: The app's theme follows the Catholic liturgical seasons — purple for Advent and Lent, white for Christmas and Easter, and green for Ordinary Time.
- Beautiful Interface: Clean, prayerful design with adjustable font size and light/dark themes.
- Offline and Private: All prayer content is stored locally. No internet connection needed. No accounts, no tracking, no data collection.
PRIVACY
Sancta Via apps collect no personal data. Everything is stored locally on your device. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. See our privacy policy for details.
