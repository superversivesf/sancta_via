Privacy Policy — Sancta Via Apps

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy covers all Sancta Via applications: Sancta Via Bible, Sancta Via Examen, Sancta Via Devotionals, Sancta Via Calendar, Sancta Via Novena, Sancta Via Prayer, and Sancta Via Saints.

The short version

  • We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. We never see anything you do in these apps.

Data collection

None. The apps contain no analytics, no advertising, no telemetry, and no third-party tracking libraries. The apps make no network requests for tracking purposes.

Data stored on your device

Content you use — bookmarks, notes, prayer commitments, reading position, reminders — is saved locally on your device only, using the device's standard local storage. It is never uploaded anywhere, and it is removed if you delete the app.

Network access

The applications are offline-first. All liturgical texts, prayers, devotional content, Bible translations, and saint profiles are bundled inside the app. No account is required, and no registration exists.

Children

Because the apps collect no data of any kind, they are safe for use by children and comply with applicable children's-privacy regulations: nothing about any user, adult or child, is gathered.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be raised via the public repository issue tracker at github.com/superversivesf/sancta_via/issues.

If these apps serve your prayer life, a small thanks at ko-fi.com/superversivesoftware is gratefully received.
