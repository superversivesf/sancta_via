Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all Sancta Via mobile applications ("the Apps"), including:

  • Sancta Via Prayer Companion
  • Sancta Via Bible Reading
  • Sancta Via Liturgical Calendar
  • Sancta Via Confession Companion
  • Sancta Via Novena Companion

Overview

Sancta Via does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The Apps are designed to work entirely offline. No data ever leaves your device.

Data We Store Locally

The Apps store the following data locally on your device using standard platform storage (SharedPreferences on Android, NSUserDefaults on iOS/macOS, and SQLite for Bible text caching):

  • Settings: Your preferences such as font size, color intensity, language, and notification settings.
  • Favorites and History: Prayers, novenas, Bible verses, and examination items you have bookmarked or recently viewed.
  • Reading Progress: Your progress through reading plans and Bible chapters.
  • App State: Onboarding completion status and other session state.

All of this data remains on your device. It is never uploaded to any server, never sent to any third party, and never accessible to the developer.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect or transmit any personally identifiable information.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting tools.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track your usage or behavior.
  • We do not request location data.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or camera.
  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not transmit any data over the network.

Notifications

The Apps may request permission to send local notifications for prayer reminders and novena day reminders. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device and do not involve any server-side processing. You can enable or disable notifications at any time in the app settings or in your device's system settings.

Children's Privacy

The Apps are suitable for all ages and do not collect any data from children or any other users. The Apps do not contain any user-generated content, social features, or communication features.

Third-Party Services

The Apps do not use any third-party services that collect data. All content (prayers, Bible texts, liturgical calendar data, novenas) is bundled within the app and accessed offline.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Using the "Clear" or "Reset" options within the app's settings.
  • Uninstalling the app, which removes all associated data.

Open Source

The Sancta Via apps are open source. The source code is available at github.com/superversivesf/sancta_via.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever need to change this privacy policy, we will update this page. Since the Apps do not collect or transmit data, we do not expect any material changes to this policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at github.com/superversivesf/sancta_via/issues.
